How a Booking Works

  1. Customer opens your link on their phone
  2. They pick a service, choose a date and select a time
  3. They enter their name, email and phone number
  4. You receive an email with all their details instantly
  5. You contact them by WhatsApp or call to confirm
💡 Tip: All time slots show as available — you confirm each booking personally. Use the admin panel to block times that are already taken.

When You Get a Booking
Page 4 of 5

You'll Receive an Email

Every booking request sends you an email immediately. It will include:

  • Customer's full name
  • Their email address
  • Their phone number
  • Which service they want
  • The date and time they've requested
  • Any special notes they added

What To Do

Contact the customer on WhatsApp or by phone to confirm the appointment. They've already been told: "You'll receive a confirmation shortly." So they're expecting your message.

If You Can't Take That Slot

Simply message the customer and offer them another time. To stop that slot showing in future, block it in your admin panel under Block Time.

💡 Tip: Reply to booking requests within 1–2 hours. Clients who get a fast response are much more likely to become regulars and leave 5-star reviews.
📧 Check your spam folder when you first set up. Add owner@salon.co.za to your email contacts so booking notifications don't get filtered.
